01 / Overview

Abstract

“

ProLoop maintains explicit awareness of long-horizon execution by relating the desired state of an active subgoal to evolving execution evidence. A learned state-relation monitor identifies whether conditions are supported, unresolved, or contradicted, derives progress as ongoing, deviated, or completed, and uses that signal to continue, correct, or transition at the right time.

03 / Framework

Method

ProLoop organizes long-horizon execution as a sequence of semantic subgoals. It compares the desired subgoal state with execution evidence, classifies progress as ongoing, deviated, or completed, and adapts the executor accordingly.

Structured state construction

State-relation monitoring
