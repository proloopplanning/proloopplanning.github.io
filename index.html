Abstract

Long-horizon robotic tasks are commonly decomposed into semantic subgoals to simplify planning. However, subgoal progress in LLM-based planning is typically inferred implicitly from interaction histories. Inaccurate estimates can misguide execution and allow local errors to propagate. To address this, we propose ProLoop, a Progress-Aware Closed-Loop framework that models subgoal progress from structured relations between desired and execution states. A learned State-Relation Monitor assesses these relations, which are then aggregated to derive subgoal progress as ongoing, deviated, or completed. The resulting progress guides execution adaptation, enabling reliable progress-aware control throughout execution. Experiments on AgentBoard ALFWorld and a real-world robotic platform show that ProLoop improves success rate by 12.94% overall and by 15.46% on Hard tasks, while reducing environment interactions by 32.3%.

ONGOING

Continue execution

When a desired condition is unresolved without a contradiction, keep reasoning and acting toward the active subgoal.

DEVIATED

Correct the mismatch

A contradicted condition or invalid action triggers focused reflection and a corrective action, retaining the current subgoal.

COMPLETED

Transition with evidence

When every desired condition is supported, consolidate useful outcomes into memory and move to the next subgoal.

ProLoop · Method

Structured states.
Reliable decisions.

Compare what should be true with what execution actually establishes, under a shared task-state schema.

5State field types
3Progress states
01

Plan & construct

The LLM proposes one active subgoal and retention cues. Desired conditions and grounded execution evidence share fields for location, visibility, attributes, relations, and inventory.

02

Assess relations

A shared semantic encoder and learned MLP predict supported, unresolved, or contradicted relations for each desired condition. Their aggregation determines progress.

03

Adapt & remember

Continue, correct, or transition according to progress. Only verified completion triggers a task-relevant memory update for subsequent planning.

Lightweight relation learning

A frozen BAAI/bge-base-en-v1.5 encoder supports a lightweight MLP relation head trained with weighted cross-entropy. Qwen3.8-Max provides offline relation annotations, with a manually inspected subset; it is not an online relation judge.
