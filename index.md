## Method

ProLoop organizes long-horizon execution as a sequence of semantic subgoals. It compares the desired subgoal state with execution evidence, classifies progress as ongoing, deviated, or completed, and adapts the executor accordingly.

<div class="method-grid">
  <div class="method-card">
    <span class="method-index">01</span>
    <h3>Structured state construction</h3>
    <p>Represent desired outcomes and evolving execution evidence in a shared semantic schema.</p>
  </div>
  <div class="method-card">
    <span class="method-index">02</span>
    <h3>State-relation monitoring</h3>
    <p>Identify supported, unresolved, and contradicted conditions for explicit progress awareness.</p>
  </div>
  <div class="method-card">
    <span class="method-index">03</span>
    <h3>Progress-guided adaptation</h3>
    <p>Continue normal reasoning, trigger targeted correction, or transition between subgoals at the right time.</p>
  </div>
</div>
